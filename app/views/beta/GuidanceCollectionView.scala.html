SAO regime

The SAO regime applies to large UK companies and groups. It requires qualifying companies, under the Finance Act 2009, to appoint an SAO who is responsible for ensuring the company has appropriate tax accounting arrangements.

Each year, companies must tell HMRC who their SAO was and submit a certificate confirming whether those arrangements were in place.
