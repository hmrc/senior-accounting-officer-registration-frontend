Find out how to register to submit your Senior Accounting Officer (SAO) notification and certificate online.

Contents

  • Who must use this service
  • Who should register
  • What you will need to register
  • What happens next

Who must use this service

A company must use this service if in its previous financial year it:

  • was incorporated in the UK
  • had a turnover of more than £200 million or
  • had a balance sheet total of more than £2 billion

These are known as qualifying companies under the SAO regime.

If a company does not meet these requirements on its own, it may still be a qualifying company. This applies if, when combined with other UK companies in its group, its turnover is more than £200 million or its balance sheet total is more than £2 billion.

Read more about aggregation of turnover in the HMRC Senior Accounting Officer internal manual (opens in new tab)

Who should register

Anyone from your organisation can register to use this service, including the SAO.

Choosing a company to register

You may nominate one UK company in your organisation to create an account. The nominated company will use the account to submit notifications and certificates for the qualifying companies.

Organisations with more than one SAO

If your organisation has more than one SAO, you can either register once for all SAOs or separately for each SAO.

Example: If your organisation has 3 SAOs, you can register once and manage all 3 submissions within a single SAO account, or register separately for each one.

Read more examples in the HMRC Senior Accounting Officer internal manual (opens in new tab)

What you will need to register

To register, you need to provide the:

  • nominated company name
  • Company Registration Number (CRN)
  • Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR)
  • contact details of the person authorised to make the submission
  • organisation Government Gateway ID (not an individual or agent account).

You can register and access the SAO service through your HMRC Business Tax Account (BTA).

If you do not have an organisation Government Gateway ID or HMRC Business Tax Account (opens in new tab), you'll need to set one up first.

Start now

What happens next

You can find a link to the digital SAO service on your HMRC business tax account. From there you can manage your account and make a submission.
