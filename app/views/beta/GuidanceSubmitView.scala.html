Find out how to submit your Senior Accounting Officer (SAO) notification and certificate online.

Contents

  • When to submit
  • What happens if you submit late
  • What you will need
  • How to submit a notification and certificate

When to submit

After the financial year ends, you must submit a notification and a certificate, including for dormant or liquidated companies.

The deadline is:

  • 6 months after the end of the financial year for public limited companies
  • 9 months after the end of the financial year for other companies

What happens if you submit late

An SAO may be charged a £5,000 penalty if a certificate is late, missing, or contains careless or deliberate inaccuracies. A group may also be charged £5,000 if it does not notify HMRC of the SAO for the financial year before the deadline.

More information

For detailed information about penalties you can read the Senior Accounting Officer internal manual..

What you will need

Before you can make a submission, you will need to have completed your registration for the Senior Accounting Officer digital service (opens in a new tab)

Additionally, after you have registered, you can access the service by logging into your HMRC business tax account to make your submission.

How to submit a notification and certificate

To make a submission you will need to:

  • sign into the SAO service to submit your notification and certificate
  • download and complete a submission template
  • list all companies the SAO was responsible for during the financial year
  • provide the SAO details and confirm whether each company has a qualified or unqualified certificate

You must be the SAO or be authorised by the SAO to make a submission.

If you choose to submit for more than one SAO, you can complete a separate submission for each SAO through the same account.
